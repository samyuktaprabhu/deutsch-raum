HOW TO USE THIS CLASSROOM

Your 90-minute study session

Use this rhythm for each weekday class. Keep your textbook and audio materials nearby.

Suggested class flow

  • 10 min — vocabulary recall and spaced revision
  • 20 min — grammar focus with English explanations
  • 20 min — Netzwerk neu B1 textbook work
  • 15 min — reading or listening practice
  • 15 min — speaking or writing output
  • 10 min — self-check, corrections and homework

What to practise

  • Say new words aloud and write your own examples
  • Complete the matching exercises in your book
  • Keep a mistake log and revisit it weekly
  • Practise speaking without writing a full script first
